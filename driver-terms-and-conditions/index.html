These Driver Terms and Conditions govern your access to and use of the EB-Driver Android application and the Enjoy Badagry ride and food-delivery services made available through it. They are separate from the customer Terms of Service and must be read with the Driver Privacy Policy.

Important: This page records the current operational rules visible in the reviewed software. Commercial choices that the implementation does not settle—such as driver classification, cancellation penalties, payout timing and appeals—are identified for confirmation and legal review.

1. Acceptance and scope

Enjoy Badagry provides a technology platform for coordinating rides and food deliveries in the locations where the service operates. By registering for, signing in to, or using EB-Driver after being approved, you agree to follow these Terms, the applicable laws and the instructions displayed in the app.

These Terms apply to driver accounts, ride assignments, delivery assignments, communications, live tracking, earnings records and related support or administration. The app does not currently show a separate digital signature or acceptance checkbox for this agreement; use of the driver service is not a statement that such a mechanism exists.

2. Eligibility

You must be legally permitted to drive or deliver in the relevant operating area, provide accurate registration information, hold the licences and vehicle documents required by applicable Nigerian and local transport rules, and satisfy Enjoy Badagry's verification requirements. The platform's precise minimum driver age and any additional regulatory requirements should be confirmed in the final legal and operating documents; these Terms do not invent an age threshold.

You may not use another person's identity, account, registration token or documents. You must keep your licence, vehicle information, contact details and other approval information current and promptly tell us if anything changes or expires.

3. Registration and verification

Driver onboarding can require an 11-digit Driver Registration Token (DRT), a password and phone verification by one-time code. The app sends registration and login metadata such as the Android platform, device model and app version. Login uses the registered phone number and password, followed by authenticated Firebase credentials for driver API access.

Recruitment and administration workflows may require identity, address, experience, vehicle and operating information, plus supporting documents. The backend requires verified driver licence and vehicle registration documents before an application can be approved, and driver approval also checks licence validity and required identity information such as NIN. Enjoy Badagry may approve, reject or request information during review; no approval deadline is promised.

Approval does not remove your obligation to maintain accurate information. A DRT is not transferable and may be used only by the person and account for which it was issued.

4. Driver relationship

Enjoy Badagry supplies booking, assignment, communication, location, payment-record and support technology. You control whether to make yourself available in the driver app, subject to active assignments and applicable rules. You remain responsible for how you drive, deliver, maintain your vehicle, comply with law and interact with customers.

These Terms do not by themselves create an employment relationship, partnership, agency, franchise, or independent-contractor classification. The correct legal and tax characterization depends on the parties' confirmed arrangement and applicable Nigerian law and must be reviewed before final publication.

5. Driver responsibilities

  • drive and deliver safely, lawfully and professionally;
  • use only truthful account, identity, vehicle and status information;
  • keep required licences, registration, roadworthiness and other documents valid;
  • follow accepted pickup, destination and delivery instructions and update statuses accurately;
  • treat customers, restaurant staff and other road users respectfully, without harassment or discrimination;
  • protect customer addresses, phone numbers, messages and other information and use them only for the service;
  • keep the app, phone, GPS and network connection reasonably available while accepting an assignment; and
  • report accidents, safety issues, suspected fraud, lost property and material service problems promptly.

6. Rides

EB-Driver can present available ride requests containing operational information such as pickup, destination, passenger and fare details. You may accept or decline a request through the app. Acceptance claims the assignment for you and triggers the platform's assignment and customer-notification workflow.

After acceptance, use the app statuses in sequence: travel toward pickup, mark arrival, confirm passenger pickup, and complete the ride only after reaching the destination and fulfilling the service. The backend prevents completion from an invalid status and checks that the booking remains assigned to you. Do not begin an unassigned or fraudulent ride, alter a destination for personal advantage, or mark a service complete when it was not completed.

Customers may receive your name, photo, phone number, vehicle type and live location for an accepted or active service. Use the displayed route and customer instructions lawfully; if a route or destination must change for safety or practical reasons, communicate clearly and record the issue through support where available.

7. Food delivery

EB-Driver can show assigned food orders and supports accepting or declining an assignment, collecting the order, marking it picked up and marking it delivered. Delivery records can include restaurant and recipient addresses, phone numbers, coordinates, instructions, landmarks, order items, charges and status history.

Collect the correct order, handle it reasonably during transport, follow lawful delivery instructions, contact the recipient only for the delivery, and complete the status only when delivery has occurred. If an order, address, recipient, restaurant, payment status or safety condition is unclear, do not fabricate completion; use the available support channel and follow the operational instruction provided.

8. Earnings and platform fees

Ride fares are calculated by the backend from configured vehicle pricing, distance-related charges, booking and service fees, passenger adjustments and any valid customer discount. The fare actually recorded for a completed ride is the source used for settlement; the app displays earnings and trip history.

Ride settlement applies a configurable ride commission percentage from platform settings. The current code default is 0%, but authorised administrators can change the setting within the permitted range for future transactions. Each transaction records the amounts used. This page intentionally does not promise a permanent percentage. The applicable rate and any customer-facing fee breakdown should be confirmed in the driver onboarding or in-app commercial information before final publication.

Food orders have configurable customer-side packaging, platform, service and delivery fees. The reviewed implementation does not establish a separate fixed driver commission or a guaranteed food-delivery payout formula. Drivers must rely on the amount shown in the assignment or earnings record and ask support about a disputed amount.

9. Wallets and withdrawals

Completed ride earnings are credited to the driver record and appear in the driver app's home, wallet, earnings, weekly-summary and transaction views. Records can include daily, weekly, monthly and lifetime totals, trip fares, driver earnings and balances.

Backend administration supports driver bank-account records, provider verification, active/default account status and withdrawal requests. Full account numbers are protected in the newer bank-account flow and administrative responses are masked. Paystack is used for bank-account verification where configured. Withdrawals may be created, reviewed, approved or rejected by authorised administrators; the code does not establish instant withdrawals or a guaranteed processing time.

Provide accurate payout information through the approved workflow, keep it current and report an incorrect balance or payment promptly. A legitimate outstanding balance is not automatically forfeited by suspension, but lawful deductions, corrections, reversals, unresolved disputes or required holds may affect when it is payable; the specific rule requires business and legal confirmation.

10. Cancellations and failed services

A driver may decline an offered ride or food assignment before accepting it. When a ride is declined, the assignment is released and may be offered again. After acceptance, the booking remains assigned until the platform or an authorised workflow changes it; do not abandon an accepted service without promptly reporting the reason.

Customer cancellation, driver unavailability, failed pickup, failed delivery, technical failure or safety concerns may change the booking status. The customer backend can refund an eligible cancelled ride to the customer's wallet, while delivery and ride records retain status and timeline information. This page does not impose a driver cancellation fee, automatic penalty or guaranteed compensation because the reviewed code does not define a complete published penalty schedule.

Do not falsely mark arrival, pickup, delivery or completion. Explain cancellation or failure facts accurately so support can investigate and handle any refund or dispute.

11. Location tracking and permissions

Location permission is required for live ride tracking. During eligible ride states, EB-Driver starts a foreground location service and uploads GPS fixes approximately every ten seconds, including coordinates, accuracy, speed, heading and timestamps.

Tracking can continue when the tracking screen is closed, the app is swiped away, the process is recreated, the phone reboots or the app is updated if an eligible ride remains. It stops when the ride is no longer eligible, permission is unavailable, access is lost or an explicit completion/stop path is used. Customers can see current coordinates only for accepted or active services, and authorised administrators can monitor operational locations.

You must not spoof, manipulate or intentionally obstruct location data for an assignment. If you deny location permission, turn off location services or lose GPS/network access, location-dependent assignments and status updates may fail. More detail appears in the Driver Privacy Policy.

12. Customer communication

The driver app supports ride chat, message read states and, where provided, text or image messages. It also allows the Android system dialler or SMS composer to be opened for contact actions; the app does not provide call recording. Use the minimum contact necessary to complete the assignment, communicate respectfully, and do not move a customer to an unofficial payment or booking arrangement.

Do not share, copy, sell or misuse customer phone numbers, addresses, messages, photos, delivery instructions or travel information. Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against or sexually exploit any customer.

13. Ratings, complaints and reports

Customers can submit a one-to-five-star rating, an optional comment and a report about issues such as unsafe driving, rude conduct, harassment, detours or vehicle condition. Ratings, reports and support tickets may be reviewed by authorised administrators alongside booking, chat and status records.

Enjoy Badagry may contact you, request information, correct a record, restrict an assignment or suspend an account while investigating. The reviewed system provides investigation and administrative decision tools but does not establish a guaranteed formal appeal process. You may send relevant facts to support when responding to a complaint.

14. Prohibited activities

You must not:

  • create or use a false identity, share an account or transfer a DRT or driver account;
  • accept fraudulent bookings, create fake deliveries, manipulate statuses or submit false trip information;
  • spoof location, interfere with GPS, WebSockets, notifications, APIs or other platform systems;
  • misuse customer data, make unauthorised recordings, or contact a customer for an unrelated purpose;
  • request unauthorised cash, alter a fare, divert a payment or manipulate wallet or payout records;
  • harass, threaten, discriminate against, assault or sexually exploit any person;
  • transport prohibited items or perform unlawful, unsafe or unlicensed transport activity; or
  • upload malware, bypass security, abuse another person's credentials or help another person evade platform controls.

15. Account suspension and termination

Access may be restricted, suspended or deactivated for inactive or invalid verification, expired documents, unsafe conduct, fraud, account sharing, serious complaints, failure to follow assignment rules, unlawful activity, technical abuse, or other material breach. The backend explicitly blocks suspended or inactive driver accounts, and administration can approve, reject, suspend, edit, review documents and manage driver access.

Restrictions may be temporary while facts are checked or permanent where continued access is unsafe or unlawful. We will not state that all earnings are confiscated. Legitimate outstanding amounts remain subject to verification, corrections, refunds, disputes, lawful holds and the applicable payout process. On termination, stop using the account and return or delete customer information as required.

16. Vehicle and operating duties

You must own, lawfully possess or be authorised to use the vehicle used for the service and keep it roadworthy, clean enough for the service, properly maintained and suitable for the selected vehicle type. Keep registration, licence, insurance and other documents required by law current. Fuel, maintenance, repairs, taxes, permits, insurance and ordinary operating expenses are not promised to be paid by Enjoy Badagry unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise.

Do not use a different vehicle from the approved record, conceal a material defect or carry a passenger or order in an unsafe vehicle. Update vehicle information when it changes and comply with any applicable capacity and road rules.

17. Safety and emergencies

Prioritise road safety over app actions. Do not use the phone while driving except through a lawful hands-free method and only when safe. In an accident, medical emergency, threat or immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service first, then notify Enjoy Badagry support as soon as reasonably possible.

For unsafe passenger behavior, an inaccessible location, a suspected dangerous delivery or another serious incident, move to a safe place, avoid confrontation and use the platform's support channel when available. Enjoy Badagry does not promise emergency dispatch, police response, insurance coverage or physical rescue unless separately confirmed.

18. Data protection and privacy

Driver personal information, location, documents, communications, ratings, operational records and account data are handled as described in the separate Driver Privacy Policy. That policy explains customer visibility, background tracking, service providers, security, retention, rights and the current account-deletion process. You must also protect customer personal information and use it only to provide the assigned service.

19. Platform availability and technical issues

Use of EB-Driver depends on a compatible device, battery, location services, internet access, GPS signals, notification delivery and third-party systems. Requests, GPS positions, chats, status changes and notifications can be delayed, inaccurate, unavailable or duplicated. The platform may be maintained, changed, rate-limited or temporarily interrupted.

Do not treat an app display as permission to drive unsafely or disregard a lawful instruction. Preserve relevant details and contact support if a technical issue affects an accepted ride, delivery, status, location or payment record.

You are responsible for your conduct, vehicle, driving, delivery handling, legal compliance, customer interactions and information submitted through your account. You are responsible for losses caused by fraud, misuse, negligence, unlawful conduct or breach of these Terms, subject to applicable law.

Enjoy Badagry is responsible for providing the platform with reasonable care, but does not guarantee uninterrupted service, a particular number of requests, customer behavior, road conditions, GPS accuracy, third-party availability or a particular level of earnings. Nothing in these Terms excludes a right or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Raise operational, payment or safety disputes with support@enjoybadagry.online with the booking or order reference and relevant facts. The platform may review records and communicate a decision. No arbitration, mandatory mediation, exclusive court, or guaranteed appeal procedure is created here without confirmation and legal review.

These Terms are intended to operate under the laws of Nigeria, subject to confirmation of the legal entity operating Enjoy Badagry and the appropriate jurisdiction. We have not inserted a registration number, registered address or exclusive court clause because those details are not verified in the project.
