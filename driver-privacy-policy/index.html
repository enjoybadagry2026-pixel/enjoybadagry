This Driver Privacy Policy explains how Enjoy Badagry processes information about drivers who register for or use the EB-Driver Android application and related driver services. It is separate from the customer-facing Privacy Policy. Please read it together with the applicable Terms of Service.

1. Scope and operator

EB-Driver connects approved drivers with ride and food-delivery opportunities on the Enjoy Badagry platform. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to Enjoy Badagry. This policy covers information collected through the EB-Driver app, the supporting API and operational tools used to provide the service.

The public website does not itself collect driver location or operate the driver account. Drivers should contact us before submitting a privacy request if they are unsure which account or record their request concerns.

2. Information we collect

Depending on what you provide and which features you use, the platform processes:

  • Account and contact data: driver registration token, full name, email address, phone number, password-related authentication data, account status, verification status and session/device records.
  • Profile data: profile photograph, gender, date of birth, nationality, state or LGA of origin, residential address and emergency-contact name, phone number and relationship.
  • Licensing and identity fields: driver registration or DRT number, driver's licence number and expiry date, and NIN or BVN values where they are present in the driver record. The current EB-Driver app displays these fields, but its reviewed code does not contain a flow for uploading scans of identity documents or licences.
  • Vehicle data: vehicle name, make or model, registration/plate number, colour and vehicle type.
  • Operational data: online and availability status, ride and delivery assignments, pickup and destination details, status events, navigation-related route data, trip history, fares, driver earnings, ratings, reviews, reports and support records.
  • Communications: ride chat text, image or reply data, read status and timestamps, plus phone numbers used by the Android system dialler when you choose to call a customer.
  • Device and diagnostic data: device model, Android platform, app version, Firebase identifier and push-notification token. The app also writes local crash reports containing exception messages and stack traces.

The reviewed driver app did not show collection of contacts, advertising ID, IMEI, microphone recordings, call recordings or SMS contents.

3. Verification and driver records

Registration and phone verification use a driver registration token, password and one-time verification codes. Login uses the driver's phone number and password, and the app uses Firebase Authentication tokens for authenticated API requests.

Verification information is used to decide whether a driver may use operational features. Some identity, licence, NIN, BVN and vehicle values are returned from the backend driver profile and may be reviewed by authorised operations staff. The recruitment and administration workflows can also receive licence, vehicle-registration, identity-card, insurance and other supporting documents, including JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF files. The reviewed EB-Driver app itself did not contain a flow for uploading document scans; its profile screens display selected verification fields.

Bank and payout records are maintained in the platform's backend for eligible driver accounts. The reviewed EB-Driver app displays earnings, wallet and withdrawal history but did not contain a bank-account entry or withdrawal API; do not send bank details through chat or another unofficial channel.

4. Location and background tracking

When tracking starts

EB-Driver requests fine or coarse location permission for live trip tracking. During an eligible ride—driver arriving, arrived at pickup, passenger picked up, trip started or in progress—the app can start a location foreground service. It obtains GPS fixes at approximately ten-second intervals and sends the newest available fix to the backend approximately every ten seconds.

What is sent and who uses it

Location updates can include booking ID, latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, heading and timestamp. Location supports assignment, navigation, customer tracking, safety, status updates and operational support. Customers can receive the driver's current coordinates only for an accepted or active ride; the customer app polls the backend and displays the driver, pickup and destination on a map. Authorised administrators can also view active driver locations for operations.

Background and stopping

Tracking is not limited to the visible tracking screen. The foreground service continues when that screen is closed and is designed to recover if the app process is recreated, the app is swiped away, the device reboots or the app is updated, provided an eligible ride remains and location permission is available. A persistent notification is used while the service is active, although tracking can continue if notification permission is denied. The service stops when the ride is no longer eligible, access is lost, permission is unavailable, or an explicit stop/completion path is used.

If you deny or revoke location permission, or turn off location services, live tracking cannot operate and the driver app may not be able to perform location-dependent ride functions. Location permission is not used by this policy to claim that the app tracks you continuously when you have no eligible ride.

5. How we use driver information

  • create and secure your driver account and maintain authenticated sessions;
  • verify eligibility, identity and vehicle information and manage account status;
  • offer, assign and record rides and food deliveries;
  • provide navigation, live tracking, customer-driver communication and delivery coordination;
  • calculate and display fares, earnings, wallet balances and transaction history and administer payouts;
  • send ride, delivery, account and earnings notifications through push messaging;
  • investigate complaints, ratings, reports, fraud, safety and misuse;
  • provide customer support, troubleshoot failures and improve reliability; and
  • comply with lawful requests and protect the platform, drivers, customers and the public.

6. What customers can see

For an accepted or active ride, customers can see the driver's name, profile photograph, phone number when available, vehicle type and live location. They can call through the phone number shown by the app, chat with the driver during supported ride statuses, and see pickup/destination and trip progress.

For food deliveries, the customer app can show the rider's name, phone number and delivery location information. Customers can submit a one-to-five-star rating and an optional comment, and can report a driver or delivery issue. These ratings, reports and related booking records are retained as platform records; they are not a promise that every customer-facing view will show every internal driver field.

Customers do not receive your licence, NIN, BVN, password, bank details, emergency-contact details or internal verification records through the documented customer app flows.

7. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed for the purposes described here, including:

  • Customers: the operational driver profile, contact details, vehicle type and live ride or delivery location described above.
  • Service providers: Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging support authentication and push notifications; Mapbox supports maps and routes; Google Play Services provides Android location services; Google Maps may open for navigation; Paystack supports payment and bank-account verification; Twilio delivers driver verification SMS where enabled; Cloudflare R2 stores uploaded images and recruitment or verification documents; Resend supports recruitment email workflows; and an external routing provider may calculate road routes.
  • Operations and support: authorised Enjoy Badagry administrators and support personnel may access driver profiles, active locations, trips, ratings, support communications, earnings, bank-account records and audit information according to their permissions. Administrative roles control access to driver data and actions are intended to be audit-recorded.
  • Legal and safety recipients: information may be disclosed where required by law, lawful process, or reasonably necessary to address fraud, safety, security or a dispute.

Provider availability and configuration can change. We do not claim that every provider listed above receives every category of information, or that an optional provider is active in every environment. The privacy and access configuration of stored document URLs and provider logs should be confirmed for the production environment.

8. Storage and security

Production app requests are configured to use HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections. Driver API requests use authenticated Firebase bearer tokens, and backend routes apply driver authentication and account checks. Administrative access is permission-controlled and platform actions are logged.

The implementation also stores some session metadata, active ride context, FCM metadata and chat data in Android SharedPreferences, and crash logs in app files, without application-level encryption in the reviewed code. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure; keep your password and verification codes private, update the app from trusted sources, and report suspected account misuse promptly.

9. Retention

The reviewed application and backend code do not establish a complete public retention schedule for driver accounts, identity fields, location history, chats, ratings, support records, financial records or audit logs. We therefore do not invent fixed deletion periods in this policy.

Records may be kept while needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet accounting or legal obligations, investigate safety matters, or enforce agreements. Location updates and active-ride records may remain available to the platform after a live ride for operational, support or audit purposes; the exact retention period requires confirmation from Enjoy Badagry. Ask us about the records relevant to you.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may ask for access to personal information we hold about you, correction of inaccurate information, information about processing, or deletion/restriction where applicable. You may also withdraw or manage Android location and notification permissions, although disabling location may prevent live-trip features.

Use the profile controls in EB-Driver where available, or contact support for a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information that we are required or permitted to keep. The app does not currently provide an automated privacy-request dashboard.

11. Account deletion

The current EB-Driver security screen labels account deletion as “Coming Soon”; the reviewed app does not provide a working self-service deletion API. To request closure or deletion, contact support@enjoybadagry.online or kingnewmanlive@gmail.com with enough information for us to identify the driver account.

Closing an account does not necessarily erase records that must be retained for safety, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, payouts, legal compliance or audit purposes. We will explain any applicable limitation when responding.

12. Eligibility and children

EB-Driver is intended for approved adult drivers who can legally provide transport or delivery services. The driver-specific minimum age and eligibility requirements should be confirmed in the applicable driver terms and onboarding materials. We do not knowingly invite children to register as drivers. If you believe a child’s information was submitted, contact us promptly.

13. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the EB-Driver app, platform operations, service providers or legal requirements change. We will update the “last updated” date, publish the revised version on this page and, where appropriate, notify drivers in the app or through account communications. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future processing, subject to applicable law.

14. Contact and legal review

For questions, access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, email support@enjoybadagry.online, email kingnewmanlive@gmail.com, or use Help & Support inside the Enjoy Badagry app. You can also visit our contact page.

This page is an operational description based on the reviewed applications and backend.
